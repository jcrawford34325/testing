About

My academic and professional interests have consistently centered on understanding human behavior and the systems that shape how people interact with the world around them. My undergraduate studies in psychology established an interest in human behavior, which I have since expanded through business education, operational experience, and independent exploration of systems and product design.

I approach business as an interdisciplinary field, connecting consumer behavior, organizational processes, technology, and design. My developing interest in prototyping and physical product development reflects this perspective: an interest in understanding how ideas translate into practical solutions and how people interact with the products and environments around them.
